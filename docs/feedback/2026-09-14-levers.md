# Lever map — playtest 2026-09-14 (F1, F2, F3, F7, F8)

Merged from the three module readers (balance, buildings, upgrades — read-only analyses of the
working tree) and the human-profile sim run. Source items: `docs/FEEDBACK.md`; contract:
`docs/DESIGN.md` "Late game contract". Player snapshot: `docs/feedback/2026-09-14-idle-stats.png`
(city 6, 1h32m, $130B, 1.53M pop, power 127M cap / 36.1M MW demand = 3.5×, happiness 169 %,
unemployment 43 %).

Conventions: "cap/demand" = `powerCap / powerDemand`; "sticker" = the catalogue value in
`src/buildings/data.js` before any mod, synergy or strain; "payback" = price ÷ steady-state
marginal income at purchase (greedy bot, pop forced to housing on both sides); "∞" = zero measured
gain. All bot numbers are from `logs/sim-gauntlet.json` (greedy, 34 foundings, legacy 530,718)
unless marked *human* (`logs/sim-human.json`). Per-module ownership follows CLAUDE.md: a lever
outside the item's owning module is flagged **(integrator)**.

Honesty notes shared by all readers: the human save is not available, so nothing below is measured
on the player's fleet; charter perks are signed at tick 0 of a replay (income 0) so their marginal
gain is quoted from `src/upgrades/data.js`, not measured; the upgrades reader's lever table arrived
truncated in the hand-off — its rows below are reconstructed from the balance and buildings readers'
cross-module citations of the same symbols.

## F2 — power runaway (P1)

**Mechanism.** `powerCap = Σ count·powerGen·byBuilding.power·mods.power` and
`powerDemand = Σ count·powerUse·strain·mods.demand` (`src/resources/index.js:288-304`). The
catalogue itself is not the source of surplus: a sticker-only equal-count fleet reads cap/demand
2.4× at N=1 and falls to 0.84× (N=50) and 0.23× (N=200) because the tier-4 strain rule makes demand
quadratic in count. The surplus is the upgrade stack folded into `mods` every tick: global power
×1.25 → ×5 (Dyson Swarm, city 4) → ×10 (Grid Charter, city 6) → ×30 (Orbital Solar, city 10) →
×90 (Energy Charter, 28) → ×270 (Helios, 30), and global demand ×0.8 → ×0.176 after the four late
demand cuts (cities 26–29): product ×1,531. Per-plant rungs (coal ×1.5, solar ×2, nuclear ×2) and
the fusion/elevator synergies (×3 at cap) sit on top; fusion is ~82 % of capacity from city 3.
Nothing in the stack is conditional on surplus. Measured (bot): cap/demand 1.00 at end of city 3,
7.2 at end of city 6 (player 3.5 — the bot over-buys generators, `bot.js:96`), 17.9 city 10, 79 city
28, 815 at 12 h; under-power share per city 33 / 22 / 25 / 22 / 14 / 9 / 2 / 1 / 0.6 % … 0 from
city 22. Lights Out (`src/simulation/milestones.js:257-266`) is reachable only in the first city.

## F3 — Megastructures unemployment cliff (P1)

**Mechanism.** `employed = min(pop, jobs)`; unemployment × 0.35 (`config.js:319`) off happiness;
income × (0.5 + 0.5h). Housing rungs and jobs rungs are asymmetric: global `mods.housing` reaches
×1.875 by city 1 (Zoning, Homestead, Tourism) and ×3.75 at Megastructures (city 15), while
`mods.jobs` stays ×1.25 (Modern Curriculum) until City Archives ×1.25 (19) and Civic Charter ×1.5
(20). Arcology Gardens (city 16) doubles arcology housing — the arcology is 78–94 % of the housing
column in cities 4–24 — while adding only ×1.5 to its 500 jobs (0.08 jobs per bed at synergy caps).
Because pop fills housing within seconds (F7), each housing rung is an instant unemployment jump.
By city 31 the global stack is housing ×37.5 vs jobs ×9.2. Measured (bot) end-of-city
unemployment: 0 % cities 8–14, 9 % (15), 47 % (16), 34 % (17), 23 % (18), 5 % (19), 0 % 20–24,
~10 % 25–28; steady-state marginal income of Megastructures is negative in cities 15–19 and of
Arcology Gardens in 16, 18–21, 23–24 — the player's "earned more by not buying" is literally true.
The bot recovers only through 146 Orbital Rings whose pop-sourced jobs synergy is 50–86 % of the
late jobs column.

housing (`src/simulation/index.js:390`). Bare, 95 % of a new housing gap fills in 27 s; the

tick). Every growth rung after Welcome Sign opens after the housing it would fill is already full
(Express Transit after High-Density, Welcome Center after Tourism Board, Preventive Care after

## F1 — late slowdown (P1)

price; a human feels marginal gain per dollar. A power rung's gain is 0 whenever cap ≥ demand at

replay. Placement is by city reach (`config.js:94-136`), so a price sets *which* city buys a nil rung,
never whether it is felt. The felt slowdown after city 3 is the cadence shape itself: cycles 7.7 ·
7.1 · 9.2 · 11.9 · 13.9 · 16.7 · 20.9 · 22.2 · 25 min (cities 3–11) while the only felt rungs are
AI Governance (city 9) and Planetary (11), among Dyson (4), Championship (5), Grid (6), Robotic (7),
Guild (8), Orbital Solar (10).

## F8 — late cost curves invert (P3)

**Mechanism.** `cost = baseCost·costGrowth^count·mods.cost` (`src/core/api.js:14-20`), one exponent
per def, resolved from `config.cost.tierGrowth` (`config.js:348`; fallback `src/buildings/index.js:70`,
resolver `:89-102,147`) unless pinned in `data.js`. A faster exponent on a smaller base overtakes the
slower one at `n = ln(base4/base1) / ln(g1/g4)`; for cottage vs arcology that is ln 4000 / ln
(1.18/1.112) ≈ 140. From zero money the crossover is $8e12 (residential) to $1.1e16 (power), so at
the player's $1.3e11 tier 1 still wins from zero (123 cottages vs 110 arcologies) — the inversion is
in the *marginal* unit past ~140 owned, exactly the fleet sizes the player (237 districts vs 202
shops) and the bot (181 cottages vs 249 arcologies) hold.
